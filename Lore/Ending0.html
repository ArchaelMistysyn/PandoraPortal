A Gift of Love

“Are these rings for me? So beautiful!” Their gems are forged from the mystical twin stars, once said to be lovers forced apart by God. The star of light is seen only at night in the physical world, while the star of darkness traverses the spiritual world by day. Forever apart… Their anguish manifests as misfortune as they weep against the lonely sky.

But now they are reunited. Malice has left them, and they glow with the power of rebirth. A lovely tale, isn’t it? Sadly, fantasy and reality can never come together in such a way.

The Pandora you knew is gone, but I am no less Pandora than she was. My many parts have come together to make me whole, yet they retain all our—my—memories and experiences. They battled you, each claiming me for themselves, yet you fought for all of us. For me.

Despite knowing I would never be complete again, you have chosen me as I am now. My decision is their decision. We will share with you all of our love.

In the times to come, for better and for worse, I will stay by your side.
