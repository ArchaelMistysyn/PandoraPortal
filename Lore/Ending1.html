A Gift of Love

“This ring… have you made it for me?” A legacy of my long-gone subordinates. Unable to find rest, yet free from misuse, they shall continue to serve me for eternity. A most fitting finality, don’t you think?

I shall accept this gift of eternal devotion, though I must ask— are you certain you can offer it?

You have fulfilled your promise, and I have said my farewells. Can you accept who I am, and what I have done? I am not my sister, and I never will be.

Be clear with me, Hero. What exactly are you after?

Very well. Your response warms my chilled heart. I love you too.

In the times to come… for better and for worse, I will stay by your side.
