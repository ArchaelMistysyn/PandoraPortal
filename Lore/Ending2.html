A Gift of Love

“You have chosen me?” This ring… it is made from… how did you come by these?

The world has grown so colourless… Collecting the remnants of a single teardrop is almost beyond the realm of possibility. Yet you have gathered and restored all these vivid droplets.

Is this truly for me?

Oh… I cannot even remember a time when my tears held such vibrance and hope. Hero, thank you for returning my colour. Perhaps I no longer need to cry.

In the times to come… for better and for worse, I will stay by your side.
